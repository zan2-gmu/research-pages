Research Pages

A small repository for standalone HTML pages that can be cited directly from other documents and opened in a browser by link.

Organize pages by type:

  • pages/notes/ for notes and working thoughts
  • pages/reviews/ for reviews and summaries
  • pages/references/ for reference collections
  • pages/experiments/ for demos and test pages

Use drafts/ for unfinished work and pages/shared/ for reused assets.

See README.md for the repo logic and naming rules.
